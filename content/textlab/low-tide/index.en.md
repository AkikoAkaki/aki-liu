The creative impulse has been persistently absent — another day of mounting fear. Today's condition has come back a little, but there's still a jarringly exhausted, disoriented feeling, something like insomnia, blocking me from creating. I don't want to sit with my phone in the soundless night eulogizing an insomniac's brooding passion the way I did a year ago, but I keep feeling that sunlight no longer belongs to me. Is it because I've been locking myself in my room too long.

My weight seems to have gone up a little, but the stomach problems seem to have gotten worse. I hope this is just my melodramatic imagination.

The loneliness is still gnawing at me. The familiar estrangement again — born from the disappointment of opening social apps repeatedly and finding no unread messages. The night is being stretched out again by thinking and scrolling. That's not a good sign.

I should cut the long night short and sink into the infinite stillness of dreams, the deep sea of consciousness.

In this narrow room, surrounded by the noise of the internet in my hand, how much knowledge can I actually take in... In this empty, dry space my brain is frozen by the stale air; the pain around my shoulders makes me more irritable. I've overdosed on the internet again. I need something to heal me again... Let me escape quickly from this half-asleep body and let the dark take over.

### 2022/06/26 17:01

> "You must choose an ideal height, laughably solitary, or a star to joke with, and rebel against the nebulae. Your life, irresponsible because of its sadness, mocks each of its own moments; and yet life is the pity we feel for duration, a dancing sensation of eternity, a time that transcends the self and rivals the sun..."
>
> Emil Cioran, *A Short History of Decay*, "An Objectless Existence"

This is Cioran's romanticism — the revenge of awakened thought. In the depths of grief and endless cursing, he still walks into the dirge, practicing and seeking a path through life, accepting dreams and sleeplessness on a road paved with the bricks of hell, carrying hate and betrayal, stepping out the footprints of an exile. Transcendence: what a magnificent word. Life inscribes its answer in time that exceeds philosophical meaning. I can't help but reflect on how contradictory and arrogant the idea of "an ordinary me, torn from the world" really is. I am no Lucifer — how could I elevate myself to the position of a superman who draws a line between himself and humanity.

### 2022/06/26 17:46

Any discussion eventually degrades into a gunpowder-thick war of oppositions, with unrestrained attacks launched indiscriminately against anyone or anything that doesn't conform, or else bystanders fanning the flames, broadcasting the conflict and then mocking both sides with equal cruelty. This is the evidence of what happens when humanity is handed a new refuge: they destroy it with the same ruthlessness. What sort of landscape is this so-called freedom and proliferation of opportunity that technology once promised... Is this the educational deficit I hypothesized at the beginning, or is it an inevitable flaw under a macro environment that grins and bares its teeth?
