Language experiments. Poetry, essays, criticism, and forms that fall between. "Production of my fragments. Contemplation of my fragments (correction, polishing, etc.). Contemplation of my scraps (narcissism)." (Barthes, *Du fragment au journal*) "The work proceeds by conceptual infatuations, successive enthusiasms, perishable manias. Discourse advances by little fates, by amorous fits." (Barthes, *La machine de l'écriture*)
